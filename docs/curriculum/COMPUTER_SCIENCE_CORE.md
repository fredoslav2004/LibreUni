# Computer Science Bachelor’s Core

Status: adopted 2026-08-23. Scope: the LibreUni computing core, not an accredited degree and not a replacement for a university’s general-education, laboratory, residency, or supervised-assessment requirements.

## Curriculum crosswalk

| Core area | LibreUni provision | Benchmark evidence | Deliberate boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematical and discrete foundations | External prerequisites until a source-backed LibreUni sequence exists | MIT requires Mathematics for Computer Science plus linear algebra or probability/statistics; Stanford requires 26 math units; Oxford begins with linear algebra, continuous and discrete mathematics, proof, and probability. | Calculus, linear algebra, probability, and discrete mathematics are required preparation, not represented by the malformed prior catalog. |
| Programming and language-level reasoning | Programming and Systems in C | MIT requires programming, software construction, and low-level C/assembly; Oxford begins with functional and imperative programming. | Additional languages and platform-specific development are electives. |
| Data structures and algorithms | Data Structures; Algorithms | MIT, Cornell, Princeton, Stanford, and Oxford all require algorithmic foundations. | Advanced randomized, online, and specialized algorithms remain later depth. |
| Theory | Theory of Computation | MIT requires computability/complexity; Princeton includes theory in its core choices; Oxford requires models of computation. | Logic, formal methods, and compiler construction beyond the current course are future depth. |
| Systems | Computer Architecture; Operating Systems; Computer Networks | MIT’s systems core includes computation structures and systems engineering; Princeton and Oxford make systems, architecture, operating systems, networks, and concurrency central options or core work. | Distributed systems and hardware design beyond the present courses are depth work. |
| Data and security | Database Systems; Cryptography and Security | CS2023 identifies data management and security as core knowledge areas; MIT offers database systems in its systems requirement. | AI/ML, HCI, graphics, and domain applications are intentionally postponed as electives. |
| Software engineering and synthesis | Software Engineering; a reviewed capstone | Stanford requires a senior project; Cornell requires a practicum or project; Princeton requires independent work. CS2023 identifies software engineering and professional practice as knowledge areas. | LibreUni cannot confer supervised credit; a capstone needs independent human review. |

## Admission and completion standard

A learner should enter with school-level algebra and complete the external mathematics sequence before relying on proof- or probability-heavy courses. Completion means solving unseen problems, passing each course’s assessments, and submitting a capstone for substantive human review. The site can provide a rigorous educational route; it does not claim degree accreditation or equivalence to a university bachelor’s award.
